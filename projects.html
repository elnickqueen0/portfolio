Rolliva: The Rolling Bench

Overview: Rolliva is a business project that introduces FlexiBench, an innovative public bench designed to improve comfort and convenience in public spaces. Its frame remains fixed for stability, while the seating surface can be manually rolled to allow users to move to a cooler or drier area after exposure to heat or rain. The project also promotes sustainability through the use of recycled materials and features a QR code that connects users to nearby emergency hotlines.


My Contribution: I contributed to the research and development of the business concept, including the product's features, materials, sustainability, target market, and business plan. This project helped me understand how innovation and technology can address everyday community needs.
